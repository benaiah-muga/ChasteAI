# ChasteAI Vision

ChasteAI is an open-source AI customer support infrastructure platform. It is not merely a chatbot platform. It is an extensible AI support operating system.

## What We're Building

A self-hostable, provider-agnostic platform that lets any business deploy intelligent AI-powered customer support assistants across multiple channels — while maintaining full ownership of their data, infrastructure, and configuration.

## Principles

1. **Self-host first.** Everything works locally with zero external dependencies. Cloud is optional, never required.
2. **Provider agnostic.** No lock-in to any AI provider. Swap OpenAI for Groq, Ollama, or any compatible API without code changes.
3. **Developer experience matters.** Type-safe end-to-end (tRPC + Drizzle + Zod). Fast setup. Clear architecture.
4. **Exceptional design.** The UI is not an afterthought. Every pixel communicates that this is serious infrastructure software.
5. **Modular and extensible.** Clean package boundaries. New channels, providers, and features slot in without modifying core logic.
6. **Reliable for production.** No null-pointer crashes. No broken code paths. Every feature works end-to-end.
7. **Portable across infrastructure.** Works on a laptop, in Docker, on Kubernetes, or at the edge.

## Who It's For

- Solo developers building AI-powered support for their projects
- Startups shipping customer support without a dedicated support team
- SMBs replacing manual support with AI assistants
- Enterprises deploying self-hosted AI support on-premise
- Privacy-sensitive organizations that cannot use cloud-only SaaS

## What We Are Not

- A generic SaaS chatbot platform
- A marketing site with AI features bolted on
- Tightly coupled to any single AI provider
- Designed for cloud-only deployment

## Success Looks Like

- Onboarding takes under 10 minutes
- Self-host setup takes under 15 minutes
- Contributors can onboard quickly
- Businesses trust deployment reliability
- Developers can extend functionality easily
